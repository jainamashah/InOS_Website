Why

Singapore is super-aged, and care can't keep up.

  • 21.4%of citizens are 65+ in 2026
  • 1 in 4citizens will be 65+ by 2035
  • 79,000seniors live alone, up from 58,000 in 2018

Eldercare centres have too few hands. Today's care robots can chat and comfort, but they can't step in to help, and every one needs custom software for each task.

Sources: Population in Brief 2026; MOH (2023, 2024)

What

The software that lets any care robot understand and help.

InOS is a social intelligence layer for robots that helps seniors stay physically and emotionally independent.

  • Understands intent, not just words

    Reads what a senior is trying to do from vision.

  • Helps emotionally and physically

    Steps in to support, instead of only talking.

  • Plug and play on any robot

    Software only, so it works with the robots centres already buy.
